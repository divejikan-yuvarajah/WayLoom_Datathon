## Scheduling rules

1. P0 work takes precedence over P1/P2/P3 work.
2. Do not start a phase until its master-plan dependency and gate are satisfied.
3. Optional competitive enhancements are cut first if they threaten the submission buffer.
4. The final target is deliberately earlier than the official deadline to preserve time for upload and confirmation.
5. If a target slips, record the impact and re-plan internally; never relabel a team target as official.
6. No internal target authorizes access to raw data during Phase 00.
